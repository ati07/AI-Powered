# Competitor Analysis

## Purpose

Understand the current AI SEO and SEO landscape.

Identify gaps where our product can provide unique value.

---

# Ahrefs

Strengths

* Excellent backlink database
* Strong keyword research
* Site audits
* Mature reporting

Weaknesses

* Limited AI visibility insights
* No AI recommendation tracking

---

# Semrush

Strengths

* Large SEO toolkit
* Competitor analysis
* Content marketing tools

Weaknesses

* AI visibility is not the primary focus
* Complex user interface

---

# Surfer SEO

Strengths

* Content optimization
* Keyword recommendations
* NLP-based scoring

Weaknesses

* Focuses on writing rather than AI discoverability

---

# Peec AI

Strengths

* AI search monitoring
* Brand mentions in AI responses

Weaknesses

* Limited technical SEO analysis
* Limited website crawling

---

# Profound

Strengths

* Enterprise AI visibility
* Brand monitoring
* AI search analytics

Weaknesses

* Enterprise-focused
* Expensive
* Limited accessibility for SMBs

---

# Goodie AI

Strengths

* AI search optimization

Weaknesses

* Smaller ecosystem
* Limited public information
