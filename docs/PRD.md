---

# 1. Vision

Businesses have spent years optimizing for search engines.

The way people discover information is changing. Increasingly, users ask AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity for recommendations instead of searching manually.

Businesses currently have no reliable way to measure, monitor, or improve how AI systems understand and recommend their content.

Our mission is to build the operating system for AI Visibility.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Current SEO tools answer questions like:

* Why am I not ranking on Google?
* Which keywords am I missing?

They do not answer:

* Why doesn't ChatGPT recommend my company?
* How well do AI models understand my website?
* What changes would improve AI visibility?
* Which competitors are more visible to AI assistants?

Businesses need a new category of tooling focused on AI discoverability.

# 4. Value Proposition

Instead of simply auditing websites, our platform continuously measures and improves AI visibility.

Users receive:

* AI Visibility Score
* Website health analysis
* AI-specific recommendations
* Technical SEO insights
* Actionable improvements
* Historical trends

---

# 5. MVP Goals

The first version must allow users to:

* Create an account
* Create an organization
* Add websites
* Crawl websites
* Analyze website structure
* Generate AI Visibility Score
* View recommendations
* Export reports

Anything outside this list is out of scope for V1.
